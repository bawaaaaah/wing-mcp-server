## Strip icons

The dashboard's strip icons (one per WING icon id) are original line drawings made for this
project, in `web/src/wing-icons/line-icons.ts` (source and render scripts in `tools/wing-icons/`).
They are MIT-licensed like the rest of the repository. They were drawn after the objects the
console's own icon grid shows (which instrument, which point of view, which letter); no outline was
traced or copied from Music Tribe's artwork.
